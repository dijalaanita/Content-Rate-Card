The same process every month once we're set up, so nothing depends on back-and-forth to keep moving. A few common questions below too.

How It Runs
Monthly cycle
Calendar first
I send a content calendar for the month, every idea tied to a reference, before anything is filmed.
Shoot, batched
Raw footage captured over one or two planned shoot days, not spread across the whole month.
Edit & grade
Colour grading, typography, pacing, and music all matched to your brand, not a generic trend preset.
Deliver in batches
Content lands on a set weekly cadence, with a short window for revision requests.
Recap & adjust
A short monthly check-in on what performed, folded into the next month's calendar.
Good Questions
Is payment upfront?
Yes, 100% upfront each month before that month's content work begins. This is non-negotiable across every package.
Do you handle paid ads or boosting?
Not inside a standard package, it's a listed add-on. Ad spend itself is always billed separately, direct to the platform.
What if we want more than the package includes?
Extra content is billed per the add-on rates, confirmed with you before I start on it. No surprise invoices.
Can we cancel anytime?
With 14 days' written notice before the next monthly cycle, per the service agreement. Content already in production for the current month isn't refunded.
Do you travel outside Lagos?
Case by case, travel costs beyond standard local transport are discussed and agreed before the shoot.
A note on how I work
My current longest-running client is a wellness café + Pilates studio pair in Lekki, two entities, one retainer, each with its own visual identity (one warm and earthy, one cool and matte) so neither brand gets flattened into the other. That's the standard I bring to every account: content that could only belong to you, not a trend with your logo pasted on top.

All packages include a discovery call before the first month to align on tone, aesthetic, and shoot dates. Custom scopes and one-off shoots available on request.
